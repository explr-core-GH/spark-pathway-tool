# Ohio 6-12 Career Advising Alignment for EXPLR Pathways

Oct 12, 2026 · Jordan Seigler, EXPLR

## Purpose and the Ohio requirements

EXPLR Pathways' advising checkpoints for grades 6-12 should line up with Ohio's Model Student Success Plan so that a student's Pathways record can be exported as a district-recognized Student Success Plan and so a district can point to Pathways as its documentation under the career advising law. This document gives the Ohio requirements grade by grade, maps Ohio's student-plan fields to Pathways fields, defines the checkpoint schedule, and ends with an implementation spec for Claude Code.

**What Ohio requires.** [ORC 3313.6020](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-3313.6020) makes every district adopt a career advising policy, updated at least every two years and posted publicly, with ten components. The ones Pathways can satisfy or document:

- A career advising plan for **grades 6 through 12**
- Grade-level examples that link schoolwork to career fields
- **Student Success Plans** for students identified as at risk of dropping out, covering the academic path to graduation and the role of CTE, competency-based and experiential learning, with parents invited
- **Documentation of each student's career advising**, reviewable by the student, parent and future schools (written consent required for any other release)
- Information on careers requiring industry credentials, certificates or degrees, and on cost offsets (CCP, dual credit, ROTC, Ohio Guaranteed Transfer Pathways)
- Use of an online planning tool: OhioMeansJobs K-12 or an equivalent ([ODEW FAQ](https://education.ohio.gov/Topics/Career-Tech/Career-Connections/Career-Advising-Policy/Career-Advising-Policy-FAQs))

**The three Ohio model documents**

| Document | Level | What it is | Use in Pathways |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Model Student Success Plan (Dec 2017)](https://education.ohio.gov/getattachment/Topics/Career-Tech/Career-Connections/Career-Advising-Policy/Model-Student-Success-Plan.pdf.aspx?lang=en-US) | Student | Grade 6-12 grid of tools, student activities and school activities, plus the fillable student forms and a 15-item planning checklist | The template Pathways checkpoints and exports mirror (sections 2-4) |
| [Career Advising Plan Template (Oct 2024)](https://education.ohio.gov/getattachment/Topics/Career-Tech/Career-Connections/Career-Advising-Policy/Career-Advising-Plan-Template.pdf.aspx?lang=en-US) | District | Planning workbook: current-practice questions, strategies per factor, equity lens, implementation and monitoring | What a district partner fills out; Pathways supplies the "monitor" data |
| [Model Policies on Career Advising (Dec 2014)](https://static.battelleforkids.org/documents/oac/ModelCareerAdvisingPolicy.pdf) | Board policy | Two adaptable policy models covering the required components | Reference only; districts already have one |

**Framework stages.** The model plan uses the original three bands: Awareness (K-5), Exploration (6-8), Planning (9-12). ODEW's current framework page describes four phases (Awareness, Exploration, Preparation, Participation) not tied to grades. Pathways uses the grade bands for checkpoint scheduling and tags each experience with the four-phase stage ([Career Connections Framework](https://education.ohio.gov/Topics/Career-Tech/Career-Connections/Career-Connections-Framework)).

**Ohio's 15-item Planning Checklist (verbatim intent)**

1. The student has updated (and changed, if needed) the Student Success Plan in grades 6 through 12.
2. The student knows and can describe their interests and work traits.
3. The student knows and can describe their academic strengths.
4. The student knows how to locate and use career information resources.
5. The student understands Ohio's 16 career clusters.
6. The student can describe the educational options available.
7. The student knows the high school graduation requirements.
8. The student knows the educational requirements for their chosen career goal.
9. The student understands labor market trends for their chosen goals.
10. The student has discussed current educational plans and career goals with a parent/guardian and counselor/teacher.
11. The student can locate and identify local job opportunities.
12. The student can identify nontraditional career options.
13. The student knows how to navigate and complete applications.
14. The student has discussed plans for after high school with a parent/guardian and counselor/teacher.
15. The student has taken action on plans for after high school.

**Required items (7):** Contact information; Career Pathway goals; Academic Pathway Plan; Assessment record; Career-ready skills; Career exploration activities; Planning checklist.
**Recommended items (9):** Resume and cover letter; Letters of recommendation; Career narrative; Record of awards; Academic and career-related classroom work; CTE competency test results, credentials and certificates; College admission test results and transcripted HS credit; Individual Transition Plan (if applicable); Other.

## Ohio student-plan fields mapped to Pathways

Ohio's plan has seven required items and nine recommended ones. Every required item maps to a Pathways object so the export can be generated without a counselor re-keying anything.

| Ohio form | Required | Ohio fields | Pathways object and fields |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Contact Information | yes | Name, home address, phone, email, graduation year | `student.profile`: name, grad_year, email; address and phone only if the district contract requires them (SB 29 minimization) |
| Education and Training History | no | Elementary, middle, high school, postsecondary with city and state | `student.schools[]`: level, name, city, state |
| Career Pathway Goals (Career Pathway Organizer) | yes | Grade; post-HS plan checkboxes; eight questions incl. career goal(s) and whether the goal changed since last year | `career_goal` record per checkpoint: grade, primary_goal, secondary_goal, cluster, changed_since_last (bool), why, post_hs_plan enum (4-yr, 2-yr, credential, apprenticeship, military, work, undecided) |
| Academic Pathway Plan | yes | Grid of courses by subject for grades 7-12 and postsecondary years | `course_plan`: rows by subject x grade; flags for HS credit in grade 8, CTE pathway, CCP/dual credit, credential program |
| Assessment Record | yes | Columns Achievement, Career Interests, Career Skills and Values, Other; each with date, tool, results, comments | `assessment` record: type enum (interest, learning_style, strengths, work_values, achievement, other), tool, date, result (JSON), summary; EXPLR RIASEC result stores here as type=interest, tool="EXPLR RIASEC" |
| Career Readiness Skills | yes | Workplace skill descriptions (reliability, teamwork, etc.) with space for the student to describe their abilities | `competency_rating` records on the Readiness Seal 15 skills: skill, level (1-4), rater (self, mentor, teacher), setting (school, work, community), evidence_link; self-descriptions are stored as student reflections, mentor ratings come from verified experiences |
| Career Exploration Activities | yes | Goals or completed experiences: mentorships, internships, community service, college visits, fairs | `experience` records (the verified experience object): type, stage, dates, hours, partner, verification status, reflection |
| Additional Learning Activities | no | AP courses, dual credit, school activities, potential references, awards | `course_plan` flags plus `activity` and `award` records; references stored as contact ids |
| Postsecondary / Military / Workforce Transitions | no | Application, scholarship, FAFSA, enlistment and job-search trackers | `transition_task` checklist, grade 11-12 only |
| Planning Checklist | yes | 15 I-can statements the student checks with comments | `checklist_item` records keyed to the 15 statements, with date checked and comment; several auto-check from data (see section 5) |
| Supports | no | Names and relationships of support people; 504, IEP, RTI, ELL status with dates | `support_contact` records (name, relationship, role). Do not store 504/IEP/RTI/ELL flags in Pathways; the district keeps those |
| Approval | yes | Student, parent/guardian, teacher/counselor signatures with dates | `plan_approval` per school year: student_ack, guardian_ack, advisor_ack, each with timestamp and method |

Recommended items Pathways already produces: resume (from verified experiences and competencies, exportable to the OhioMeansJobs resume builder), career narrative (grade 12 reflection), record of awards, classroom work and exemplar artifacts (portfolio), CTE credentials and certificates (credential records). Letters of recommendation, test results and transcripted credit stay with the school.

## EXPLR Pathways checkpoints by grade

Two checkpoints per year, fall and spring, in every grade 6-12. Fall collects assessments and sets goals; spring records exploration, reflection and the course plan for next year, and produces the annual plan approval. Each checkpoint is a set of required tasks; a checkpoint is complete when every required task is done. Task names below are the identifiers Claude Code should use.

| Grade | Fall checkpoint (by Nov 15) | Spring checkpoint (by May 1) | Ohio components satisfied |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6 | `account_setup`; `learning_style_survey`; `interest_inventory` (EXPLR RIASEC, grades 5-8 form); `career_goal` (first draft, may be "exploring") | `exploration_log` (at least 2 exposure experiences: speaker, tour, challenge); `reflection` on each; `elective_plan` for grade 7; `checklist` items 2, 4, 5; `plan_approval` | Learning style; career interests; exploration activities; pathways; courses |
| 7 | `interest_inventory` (re-take or confirm); `career_goal` (revise; changed_since_last); `pathway_research` (one occupation: education required, Ohio wage) | `exploration_log` (2+, at least one Exploration-stage: interview or shadow); `reflection`; `elective_plan` incl. HS-credit options; `course_plan` started; `checklist` 2, 4, 5, 6; `plan_approval` | Career interests; pathways; courses; exploration |
| 8 | `interest_inventory`; `strengths_inventory`; `career_goal`; `fit_map` (strengths x interests, replaces the Venn diagram) | `exploration_log` (2+, at least one job shadow); `reflection`; `hs_pathway_choice` (CTE pathway, program or comprehensive) and `course_plan` for grade 9 with graduation requirements shown; `checklist` 2-8; `plan_approval` | Adds strengths/skills; postsecondary programs; diploma pathways |
| 9 | `learning_style_survey`; `interest_inventory` (9-12 form); `strengths_inventory`; `career_goal`; `credential_program_review` | `exploration_log` (2+, Preparation-stage encouraged: service learning, project, enterprise); `reflection`; `competency_self_rating` (3 Readiness Seal skills); `course_plan` grade 10; `test_prep_plan`; `checklist` 2-9; `plan_approval` | Adds test prep; internships, service learning as exploration |
| 10 | `career_profile` (interest x personality); `career_goal`; `college_admission_research` (criteria for 2 institutions or programs) | `exploration_log` (2+, at least one Participation-stage or 20+ hour experience); `resume_draft` (auto-built from verified experiences); `competency_rating` (mentor-rated where a verified experience exists); `course_plan` grade 11; `checklist` 2-11; `plan_approval` | Adds college and career planning; resume |
| 11 | `career_profile`; `work_values` (Work Importance Locator or EXPLR equivalent); `fit_map` (values x strengths x interests); `career_goal`; `postsecondary_search` (3 options compared: cost, admission, outcome) | `exploration_log` (2+, incl. a college/program visit or fair and a 40+ hour WBL experience toward the 250 hours); `resume_update`; `competency_rating` (2 settings for Seal evidence); `financing_plan` (FAFSA readiness, scholarships, CCP credit earned); `course_plan` grade 12 incl. credentials; `checklist` 2-13; `plan_approval` | Adds work values; financing; Seal evidence |
| 12 | `learning_style_survey`; `career_profile`; `work_values`; `career_goal` (final); `application_tracker` (college, work, military, scholarship, FAFSA); `career_narrative` | `exploration_log`; `resume_final`; `portfolio_compile` (exemplar work, narrative, resume, credentials); `seal_evidence_export` (if 15 skills validated across 2 settings); `wbl_hours_export` (if 250+); `transition_plan` (accepted option, start date); `checklist` all 15; `plan_approval` | Adds applications, portfolio, transitions |

**Advising conversations.** Ohio's model policies call for at least one advising meeting per year (Model 1) or one per semester (Model 2). Pathways treats each checkpoint as the artifact of one advising conversation: the advisor opens the checkpoint, reviews what the student entered, adds `advisor_notes`, and marks the checkpoint reviewed. The spring `plan_approval` is the annual signature step Ohio's form ends with.

**Experience minimums.** The exploration counts above are Pathways' floor, drawn from CMSD PACE (3-4 job shadows across 6-12), Ohio's expectation of exploration every year, and the research that breadth of exposure in middle school predicts later outcomes. A student who meets every spring minimum from grade 6 arrives in grade 12 with 14 or more logged experiences, at least two job shadows, and the 40-hour grade 11 WBL experience that starts the 250-hour pathway.

## Implementation spec for Claude Code

This section is written to be handed to Claude Code as-is. Pathways is a Lovable project (React, Tailwind, shadcn/ui, Supabase); names below are table and column names.

### Data model

```
students            id, district_id, school_id, grad_year, grade (derived), riasec_code, created_at
checkpoints         id, student_id, school_year, term (fall|spring), grade, status (open|submitted|reviewed), reviewed_by, reviewed_at, advisor_notes
checkpoint_tasks    id, checkpoint_id, task_key, required (bool), status (todo|done|waived), completed_at, waived_by, payload (jsonb)
assessments         id, student_id, type (interest|learning_style|strengths|work_values|career_profile|achievement|other), tool, taken_at, result (jsonb), summary
career_goals        id, student_id, checkpoint_id, primary_goal, cluster, post_hs_plan, changed_since_last, why
course_plans        id, student_id, grade, subject, course, flags (hs_credit|cte|ccp|credential)
experiences         (existing verified-experience table) + stage (awareness|exploration|preparation|participation), hours, verification_status, reflection
competency_ratings  id, student_id, skill (one of 15 Readiness Seal skills), level (1-4), rater_type (self|mentor|teacher), setting (school|work|community), experience_id, rated_at
checklist_items     id, student_id, item_no (1-15), checked_at, comment, auto (bool)
plan_approvals      id, student_id, school_year, student_ack_at, guardian_ack_at, advisor_ack_at, method
transition_tasks    id, student_id, kind (college|work|military|scholarship|fafsa), label, due, done_at
support_contacts    id, student_id, name, relationship, role
```

### Checkpoint generation

- On each student's enrollment and at the start of each school year, create the fall and spring checkpoints for the student's grade from a `checkpoint_template` table keyed by grade and term; the task keys and minimums are the table in section 4.
- A task is `done` when its completion rule is satisfied, not when the student clicks done. Rules:
  - `interest_inventory`, `learning_style_survey`, `strengths_inventory`, `work_values`, `career_profile`: an `assessments` row of that type dated within the current school year.
  - `career_goal`: a `career_goals` row for this checkpoint with a non-empty primary_goal ("exploring" allowed in grade 6).
  - `exploration_log`: count of `experiences` in the school year at or above the grade's minimum, with the stage condition met (e.g., grade 8 needs one `job_shadow`; grade 11 needs one experience with hours >= 40 and verification_status = verified).
  - `reflection`: every experience counted in `exploration_log` has a reflection of 50+ words.
  - `competency_rating` / `competency_self_rating`: the required number of ratings exist for the year; grade 11 requires at least two distinct settings.
  - `course_plan` / `elective_plan`: rows exist for next grade.
  - `plan_approval`: all three acknowledgments recorded for the school year.
  - Everything else: the task's payload form is submitted.
- `waived` requires an advisor and a reason; waived tasks do not block checkpoint completion but are shown on the export.

### Checklist automation

Auto-check these of Ohio's 15 statements from data and leave the rest for the student: 1 (plan updated: any checkpoint submitted this year); 2 (interests: interest assessment exists); 3 (strengths: strengths assessment exists); 5 (16 clusters: the inventory covers them); 7 (graduation requirements: grade 8+ course plan with requirements shown); 10 and 14 (discussed with parent and advisor: plan_approval acks); 15 (taken action: a transition_task done). Student-checked: 4, 6, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13.

### Exports

- **Student Success Plan PDF**: renders Ohio's forms in Ohio's order (Contact, Career Pathway Organizer, Academic Pathway Plan, Assessment Record, Career Readiness Skills, Career Exploration Activities, Additional Learning Activities, Transitions, Planning Checklist, Approval) from the tables above. One PDF per school year, generated at spring `plan_approval`, stored on the student record.
- **OhioMeansJobs resume**: plain-text and PDF resume built from verified experiences, competencies and awards, formatted for paste into the OMJ K-12 resume builder (no API exists).
- **Readiness Seal evidence** (grade 12 or on request): the 15 skills with the mentor ratings, settings and experience links, in the layout of the OMJ-Readiness Seal Validation Form, so a school can complete the official form from it.
- **WBL hours log**: experiences with verification_status = verified and stage = participation, hours summed by school year, for district EMIS entry toward the 250-hour pathway.
- **District monitoring**: per-school counts of checkpoint completion, experiences by stage, students with a verified WBL experience, disaggregated by grade and demographic fields the district supplies; this feeds Activity 4 (Monitor) of Ohio's Career Advising Plan Template.

### Access and privacy

- Students see and edit their own plan; guardians see it; advisors at the student's school see and annotate; district admins see monitoring aggregates. Release to anyone else (a receiving school, an employer) requires an explicit consent record per ORC 3313.6020(A)(7).
- Under SB 29 (ORC 3319.325-.326) the district owns the records when Pathways runs under a district contract: no sale or commercial use, return or destroy within 90 days of contract end, breach information disclosed so the district can notify parents within 72 hours. Keep PII minimal: no home address or phone unless the contract requires it; no 504/IEP/RTI/ELL flags in Pathways.
- Under-13 students (some sixth graders) require verifiable parental consent for any media in the portfolio; schools may consent only for school use (COPPA).

### Open decisions

- Whether grade 6 checkpoints run in Pathways or only from grade 7 for districts that start OMJ accounts late.
- Whether `plan_approval` uses a typed name, a click-through from a guardian email link, or district SIS sign-off.
- Which EXPLR instrument stands in for Ohio's Learning Style Survey and Work Importance Locator, or whether Pathways links out to OMJ K-12 for those.
